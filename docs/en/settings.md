# Settings

A **Setting** gives the common rules a world to inhabit: Powers, Agents, their relationships with Humanity, typical situations and any setting-specific rules that are actually needed.

The first settings currently planned are:

- [Choirs & Legions](choirs-and-legions)
- [New Gods](new-gods)
- [Signals](signals)
- [Blood & Night](blood-and-night)

They are not a shared canon. Each is a different way to play **Truchements**.
