# Settings

A **Setting** gives **Truchements** a concrete form: Powers, Agents, their relationships with Humanity, typical situations, and, when useful, a few rules specific to that framework.

The first Settings currently planned are:

- [Choirs & Legions](./choirs-and-legions.md)
- [Ancient & New Gods](./ancient-and-new-gods.md)
- [Signals](./signals.md)
- [Blood & Night](./blood-and-night.md)

They do not form a shared canon, nor do they necessarily describe separate universes.

## Combining Settings

Settings can be played on their own or **combined within the same world**.

A campaign may therefore feature Angels, Demons, Vampires, Werewolves, ancient gods, new Powers born from Money, Networks, or AI, as well as extraterrestrial or otherwise difficult-to-identify entities.

These Powers do not need to share the same origin, understand one another, or fit into a single cosmology.

The group simply decides what exists in its world and which Settings are useful to play it.

:::note[Example]

An Agent of an Archangel may investigate a series of disappearances caused by Vampires, discover that they serve an ancient Egyptian deity, and receive unexpected help from an Agent bound to a recent Power: **Communication**.

All of this can belong to the same game world.

:::
